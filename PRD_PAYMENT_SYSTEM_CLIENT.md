# Payment System — Quick Summary

**Confirmed flow:** Customer books → provider accepts → customer pays 50% deposit via GCash QR (straight to provider's own account) → job gets done → customer pays remaining 50% on-site the same way → provider has now been paid in full, directly, by the customer. The site just displays the QR — it never touches the money.

**The one thing we need from you:**
Since the platform's fee is baked into the price but the money goes straight to the provider, the platform never actually holds any of it. So collecting your fee means getting it *back* from the provider after the fact, not paying *out* to them.

👉 **How should that work?** Does the provider send your fee back manually after each job, does admin invoice them, and what happens if they just don't pay it? Right now there's no answer, which makes it an honor system.

**Other things to decide (quick answers are fine):**
- Static personal QR (payer types the amount — no registration needed, but typos happen) or dynamic QR (preset amount — safer, but is usually a GCash for Business feature)?
- How is a total price locked in for "quote"/"hourly" jobs, which don't have a fixed price at booking time?
- What's the fee — flat %, fixed peso, tiered? Shown to the customer or not?
- What if the customer never pays the deposit — auto-cancel after how long?
- Refund policy on cancellations/no-shows after the deposit is paid?
- Should the customer confirm the job is done, or is the provider's word enough (as it is today)?
- Do you need receipts/invoices generated for customers or for BIR purposes?
- Does the 50/50 split apply to every booking, or can small jobs skip it?

Send us answers in whatever format is easiest — even a quick voice note or bullet list works.
